# M2 — Static data and Sparrow

**Status:** Active — calibration passed; 203-task confirmation pending

The first adapter slice is operational: Spyrrow is pinned, progressive feasible reports are
captured, layouts beyond the physical sheet length are rejected, duplicate placements are removed,
and immutable run archives can be produced. The registered 51-task calibration completed 612 of
612 cells with 100% valid archives and selected 10 seconds per seed.

All 51 calibration tasks produced at least two distinct candidates inside the primary 0.5%
immediate-performance envelope at 1, 3, and 10 seconds. The shorter budgets failed only the frozen
best-length degradation limits: 1 second had 3.9283% median and 7.3121% P95 degradation; 3 seconds
had 1.4868% median and 3.3686% P95 degradation. M2 remains open until the 203-task confirmatory
evaluation is executed and its preregistered gate is applied.

M2 turns Sparrow from a one-answer optimizer into a reproducible research instrument. Public polygon fixtures are ingested, multiple seeded runs are executed, progressive incumbents are captured, infeasible layouts are rejected, and near-tied placements are archived.

> **Question:** Can we reliably obtain the alternative current actions that YieldForge is supposed to choose between?

## Acceptance boundary

The same manifest reproduces a stable set of feasible, near-tied but materially different placement layouts.

“Data” here means static geometry, not manufacturer history or the full temporal benchmark.
