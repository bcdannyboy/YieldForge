# M6 — Temporal benchmark data

**Status:** Passed — canonical population and lowering pilot validated 2026-08-23

M6 creates transparent timestamped demand streams from real polygon geometry. The regimes vary
recurrence, family similarity, bundles, high-mix uniqueness, and regime shifts, including a
no-signal control.

> **Question:** Do we have reproducible test worlds that isolate when future-aware remnant
> decisions should and should not help?

## Acceptance result

Yes, within the controlled synthetic-chronology boundary.

The registered contract `yfm6-3eeda3f4feb80813807c501a` binds frozen M0 contract
`yfm0-29b7efe8ac2a0a9995c4f907`, the full committed Lectra catalog, six regimes, eight common
seeds, 24 events per stream, source-recorded projection, generated rates, assumed material,
ordinary candidate requirements, and an immutable 12-stream calibration / 36-stream evaluation
split.

The canonical population is `yftp-49bd7ce5fd34b2779440c52f`:

- 48/48 registered streams generated with zero failed cells;
- 1,152 total events and 37,247 exact source-part references;
- every stream regenerated byte-for-byte from its registered seed and generator version;
- every stream met its realized regime thresholds;
- every stream appeared in exactly one preregistered partition; and
- all events lowered into 1,024 M0-compatible replay-ready batches with no source task, polygon,
  orientation, flip state, stock, or assumed material loss.

The six constructions are measurably distinct:

| Regime | Realized construction across 8 streams |
| --- | --- |
| No signal | 24 unique tasks per stream, zero shape recurrence, zero material recurrence |
| Exact recurrence | 1 task per stream, task and shape recurrence both 100% |
| Family similarity | 4–6 distinct tasks, one shared family signature, 100% shape recurrence |
| Compatible bundle | 24 unique tasks, all events in exact three-event compatible release batches |
| High mix | 24 unique tasks, no same-time bundle, 91.30% material recurrence |
| Regime shift | 12 repeated-anchor events followed by 12 unique tasks with zero task-set overlap |

Full-catalog geometry, composition, source sheet dimensions, and source-recorded orientation state
remain evidence-bound. Chronology and regime labels are generated; material is assumed; feasibility
rates are generated. Tasks `4365` and `25801` remain visibly excluded because their non-`s1`
semantics are unresolved.

## Lowering pilot and collision decision

Pilot `yfm6p-c5852afe0dd52f41689f3ed6` sampled one evaluation stream per regime:

- 144 events lowered into 128 batches and 4,769 source-part instances;
- 14,307 exact Shapely validity, area, and bounds queries found zero invalid geometries;
- measured lowering plus geometry validation took 4.083059 seconds;
- linear full-population projection is approximately 0.544408 minutes; and
- actual repeated fit/search calls were zero.

Shapely remains the correctness backend. Jagua is deferred because M6 data lowering did not contain
the repeated remnant fit/search workload needed to trigger the registered decision rule. M7 must
measure actual fit/search share and may spike Jagua only at at least 30% of end-to-end replay time
or a registered replay projection beyond 15 minutes.

## Claim ceiling

M6 proves reproducible controlled synthetic chronology over real catalog geometry, immutable
partitions, measured constructions, and lossless replay-ready lowering. It does not establish real
factory chronology, candidate richness, remnant opportunity frequency, baseline strength,
future-information value, material savings, physical recoverability, or commercial value.
