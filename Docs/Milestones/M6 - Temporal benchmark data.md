# M6 — Temporal benchmark data

**Status:** Next — preparation boundary recorded after M5 pass

M6 creates transparent timestamped demand streams from real polygon geometry. The regimes vary recurrence, family similarity, bundles, high-mix uniqueness, and regime shifts, including a no-signal control.

> **Question:** Do we have reproducible test worlds that isolate when future-aware remnant decisions should and should not help?

## Acceptance boundary

Streams reproduce exactly from their generator version and seed, their declared construction properties are measured, and calibration and evaluation partitions are immutable.

Synthetic chronology tests the mechanism; it does not establish what real factories experience.

The implementation inventory, gaps, and ordered entry plan are frozen in
`Docs/plans/2026-08-23-m6-temporal-benchmark-preparation.md`. The three committed tiny order books
remain schema fixtures until the seed matrix, partitions, additional regimes, replay adapter,
rate/stock manifests, candidate-archive requirements, and construction gates are registered.
