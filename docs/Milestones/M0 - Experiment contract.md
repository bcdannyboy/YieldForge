# M0 — Experiment contract

**Status:** Active

M0 decides what experiment we are actually running before implementation or observed results can influence the rules. It gives precise meanings to savings, future information, usable remnants, fair policy comparison, failure, and success.

> **Question:** What would count as a valid positive or negative result?

M0 exists to prevent cost terms, information access, candidate budgets, terminal assumptions, or success thresholds from drifting until the oracle appears favorable.

## Acceptance boundary

The experiment has executable definitions for its primary metric, information sets, event timing, cost accounting, candidate parity, remnant eligibility, terminal treatment, failure handling, thresholds, and claim ceiling. No economically meaningful ambiguity remains.

## What passing M0 does not mean

It does not prove that any component exists or that the YieldForge hypothesis is true.
