# M10 — Experiment and verdict

**Status:** Later

M10 runs the frozen paired experiment across declared regimes, controls, seeds, remnant rules, candidate budgets, storage assumptions, terminal treatments, and search settings. Its output is a decision, not another open-ended research backlog.

> **Question:** Has YieldForge earned another investment?

## Acceptance boundary

Results include uncertainty, ablations, negative controls, sensitivity, failure analysis, and search-quality limits. The preregistered threshold produces one of three outcomes:

- stop;
- acquire real manufacturer history; or
- authorize a narrowly bounded next research stage.

Even a green virtual verdict does not establish physical recoverability, adoption, realized ROI, integration reliability, or market demand.
