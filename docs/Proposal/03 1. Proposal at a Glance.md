# 1. Proposal at a Glance

YieldForge is best understood as a future material-decision layer, not as another CAM replacement.

| Dimension | Proposal |
| --- | --- |
| Working concept | A CAM-agnostic system that chooses among process-feasible nests according to total future material economics rather than immediate packing density alone. |
| Initial engine | Sparrow for high-quality irregular 2D candidate generation; jagua-rs for exact geometry, irregular containers, and later material-quality zones. [1][2] |
| First question | How much can perfect information save over a strong commercial-style baseline when both policies share the same candidate set and compute budget? |
| First deliverable | A deterministic chronological simulator, exact remnant inventory, baseline suite, rollout oracle, beam-search oracle, public/synthetic benchmarks, and an investment verdict. |
| MVP boundary | A bounded research implementation organized into four gated phases, with explicit non-goals and a pre-registered decision framework. |
| Preferred success signal | Target-regime oracle advantage of at least 2.5%, ideally 3% or more; at least 1.5 percentage points attributable to unknown future demand; and no-signal controls near zero. |
| Immediate non-goals | No forecasting, quotes, machine scheduling, heterogeneous material fields, CAM integration, production UI, purchasing optimization, or autonomous shop execution. |
| End-state buyer | A high-material-spend manufacturer with recurring irregular parts, meaningful remnant inventory, and a strong incumbent CAM that YieldForge complements rather than replaces. |

## One-sentence product definition

| YieldForge<br>A probabilistic material-decision layer that prices the future geometric utility of each candidate nest and selects the present action with the lowest expected cumulative material cost. |
| --- |

## Why the validation comes first

The broad end-state system would combine irregular nesting, exact remnant inventory, order batching, material allocation, stochastic demand, machine assignment, and rolling scheduling. That is a large operations-research and industrial-integration program. The perfect-information build deliberately removes every component that is not necessary to determine whether the central economic effect exists.

This sequencing protects against the most expensive failure mode: building a sophisticated forecasting and integration platform around an oracle gap that is too small to monetize. The first program is therefore an evidence-generating research MVP with reusable technical assets, not a customer-facing production system.

## The strategic distinction

| System | Primary role | What it optimizes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Sparrow | Geometric optimizer | Finds extremely compact process-feasible arrangements of irregular 2D parts. |
| Existing CAM | Manufacturing execution authority | Applies machine rules, process parameters, toolpaths, sequencing, and NC output. |
| YieldForge | Economic decision layer | Determines which batch, stock object, and candidate nest produces the best long-run material state. |
