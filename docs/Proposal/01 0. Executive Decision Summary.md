# 0. Executive Decision Summary

The proposal is to fund a falsification experiment, not to commit to a full manufacturing software build.

| Recommended decision<br>Approve the perfect-information MVP, subject to phase-by-phase kill gates. Do not authorize the stochastic production product, CAM integrations, multi-machine scheduling, or a customer-facing platform unless the MVP identifies a target regime with at least 2.5% oracle savings, at least 1.5 percentage points attributable to unknown future demand, and acceptable supporting metrics. |
| --- |

## The thesis

Conventional nesting systems are designed primarily to maximize the value of the current cut: fit the current parts tightly, select a currently available sheet or remnant, and generate process-feasible toolpaths. YieldForge asks a different question: among several current nests that are nearly tied on immediate utilization, which one creates the lowest total material cost over future orders?

The opportunity exists because equal-area leftovers are not economically equivalent. A compact nest may strand material in thin channels and disconnected pockets, while a nest that is only fractionally less dense may preserve one contiguous irregular remnant capable of satisfying a recurring future part family. The difference is not visible in current utilization alone. It is an inventory option-value problem expressed through exact geometry.

## What the market already provides

The market is not empty. AccuFit, Lantek, Plataine, SigmaNEST, ProNest, JETCAM/CrossTrack, BySoft, RADAN, SlabWise, and related systems already cover substantial portions of nesting, remnant tracking, stock selection, current-order mixing, scheduled-order lookahead, deliberate remnant shaping, production scheduling, and defect avoidance. [3]-[11] Academic literature also treats multi-period and stochastic cutting-stock problems with usable leftovers. [12]-[16]

Accordingly, YieldForge is not defensible as "software that saves remnants" or "future-aware nesting." Its potentially differentiated kernel is narrower: exact irregular-polygon remnants, multiple near-tied process-feasible candidate nests, future demand that is not yet a firm order, marginal option value conditional on all other inventory, and a chronological counterfactual system that proves the incremental value against strong as-of-time policies.

## What the MVP establishes

The MVP builds a deterministic research system around Sparrow, an open-source high-performance irregular 2D strip-packing solver, and jagua-rs, its geometry engine. [1][2] It will capture multiple Sparrow candidate layouts, extract exact residual polygons, reuse those remnants as future cutting surfaces, replay temporal order streams, and compare a strong baseline with a policy that can see every future order.

This first system contains no forecasting model. Perfect future knowledge is deliberately used to answer the prerequisite question: is there enough exploitable geometric option value to justify predicting it later? If a clairvoyant policy cannot create meaningful incremental savings, a practical stochastic system cannot rescue the business case.

## Bottom-line recommendation

The expected value is favorable because the MVP is bounded, technically separable, and capable of killing the idea before a multi-year build. The document therefore recommends completing the MVP while explicitly declining to underwrite the end-state product until the measured evidence profile clears the pre-registered threshold.
