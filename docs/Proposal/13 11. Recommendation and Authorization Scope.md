# 11. Recommendation and Authorization Scope

Approve the evidence program; do not pre-approve the product build.

## 11.1 Recommendation

Authorize the YieldForge perfect-information MVP as a gated research build. Each phase produces an independently reviewable artifact and can terminate the effort without implying authorization for the stochastic product, commercial integrations, or shop-floor deployment.

This is a rational bet because the technical work is bounded, the core artifacts are reusable, and the result can falsify the opportunity before the project incurs the much larger costs of forecasting, CAM integration, shop scheduling, customer data onboarding, and production hardening.

## 11.2 What authorization includes

- The precise scope, milestones, and tools described in Sections 5 through 8.

- Use of Sparrow and jagua-rs under pinned versions for research and benchmarking.

- Creation of exact remnant geometry, chronological simulation, strong baselines, and perfect-information policies.

- Generation and publication-ready documentation of synthetic benchmark streams derived from public irregular geometry.

- A final go/no-go report under the thresholds in Section 9.

## 11.3 What authorization explicitly excludes

- A customer-facing SaaS product or production deployment.

- A stochastic demand or quote-conversion model.

- Commercial CAM, ERP, MES, or machine integrations.

- Multi-machine scheduling, purchasing automation, or autonomous production dispatch.

- Material defect/strength fields beyond schema placeholders.

- Sales, branding, trademark filing, or external marketing under the YieldForge name.

- Any implementation beyond the MVP's defined scope without a separate decision.

## 11.4 Decision statement

| Proposed authorization<br>Proceed with Phase A of the perfect-information MVP. Continue into later phases only when the preceding acceptance gate is passed. Continue beyond the MVP only if the pre-registered quantitative and qualitative evidence profile is met. A negative result is a successful outcome if it prevents a much larger speculative build. |
