# 10. Risk Register and Mitigations

The validation is designed to expose failure early, but several technical and commercial risks remain.

| Risk | Severity | Mitigation |
| --- | --- | --- |
| No meaningful oracle gap | High / existential | Run perfect information before forecasting or integration; stop below threshold. |
| Synthetic-to-real gap | High | Limit claims; require a subsequent historical manufacturer replay before product development. |
| Strawman baseline | High | Implement multiple strong policies, freeze the best on calibration data, and use identical candidate sets. |
| Candidate-set bias | High | Run multi-seed/progressive Sparrow archives, epsilon sweeps, and an expanded-search ablation. |
| False remnant value from geometry bugs | High | Exact accounting invariants, adversarial fixtures, differential tests, and visual regression. |
| Unrecoverable residual regions | High | Report geometric upper bound and conservative operational extractor separately. |
| Horizon/terminal artifact | High | Primary result uses scrap-only or zero terminal option credit; run horizon sensitivity. |
| Oracle search weakness | Medium-high | Compare rollout, beam, and exhaustive small cases; disclose approximation gap. |
| Compute explosion | Medium-high | Cache candidates, prune stock choices, hash states, use beam diversity, and stage experiments. |
| Existing products already solve enough | Medium-high | Measure incremental advantage over a composite commercial baseline; do not rely on novelty claims. |
| Low demand recurrence | Medium-high | Use recurrence as a customer-qualification variable; accept that the product may be vertical-specific. |
| Operator nonuse of remnants | Later high risk | Not in first scope; future pilot must use conservative recovery rules and shadow-mode feedback. |
| CAM/API limitations | Later high risk | Prefer advisory mode and customer systems with candidate/export APIs. |
| Material quality complexity | Later medium-high risk | Reserve schema fields; add categorical zones before continuous structural properties. |
| Licensing and patent exposure | Medium | Keep layers modular; obtain MPL and patent claim review before distribution/scheduling. |
| Working-name collision | Low for research / medium for launch | Use YieldForge internally; conduct trademark/domain/category clearance before external branding. |

## 10.1 The most important methodological risk

The largest risk is not that the code fails. It is that the experiment returns an attractive percentage by comparing a clairvoyant policy with a weak current-state policy. The baseline must therefore include the strongest practical advantages already available to a competent shop: existing remnant selection, current order pooling, regularity preferences, age/FIFO logic, storage cost, and scheduled known orders. The oracle should differ primarily in its knowledge of later realized demand.

## 10.2 The most important commercial risk

The broad system overlaps mature CAM, MES, material planning, and specialist optimization vendors. Even a technically positive result does not imply a standalone company. Commercial value depends on whether the differentiated oracle gap can be captured through an overlay that integrates with installed systems without requiring a wholesale replacement. The validation program proves an economic kernel, not distribution, integration, or sales.
